Definition 5.1.5. Null Space of a Matrix.

Let \(A\) be an \(m \times n\) matrix. The null space of \(A\text{,}\) denoted \(\Nul A\text{,}\) is the set of vectors \(\v{x}\) such that\(A\v{x} = \v{0}\text{.}\) Symbolically speaking,
\begin{equation*} \Nul A = \set{\v{x} \in \R^n \mid A\v{x} = \v{0}} \end{equation*}
